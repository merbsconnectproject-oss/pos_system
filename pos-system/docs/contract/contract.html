2.Background

  1. 2.1The Client operates a retail business trading in lighting, electrical and hardware goods from two outlets, and requires a computerised system to record every sale, maintain accurate stock figures per outlet, reconcile cash daily, track customer credit, and give the owner a single view of the whole business.
  2. 2.2GiftedLabs has designed and built Radiance POS, a point-of-sale and inventory management system engineered specifically for Ghanaian multi-outlet retail — including mobile money payments, multi-unit selling with conversions, trade and contractor price tiers, installment credit, and operation on unreliable power and internet.
  3. 2.3The Client wishes to engage GiftedLabs to complete, deploy, configure and support that system for its two outlets, and GiftedLabs agrees to do so on the terms set out below.

Schedule A — Delivered Functionality

This Schedule is the definitive statement of what the Client is purchasing. Every module below is part of the System delivered under clause 4.1 and covered by the warranty in clause 11.4 and by lifetime support under clause 7.

A1Authentication, Roles and Access Control
Staff sign-in with username and password; outlet selection on entry; screen lock with PIN re-entry so a cashier can step away without ending a shift; idle session timeout. Four role types — Owner, Manager, Storekeeper, Cashier — governed by a full permissions matrix covering selling, discounting, voiding, price visibility, stock adjustment, cash movement, reporting, user management and settings. Per-user grants and revocations against the role baseline. Controls a user has no permission for are not displayed at all rather than shown and disabled. Manager PIN approval for privileged actions, with the approver’s identity recorded against the transaction.
A2Point of Sale Terminal
A dedicated full-screen selling terminal. Product tile grid with category filtering, live search and barcode scanning. Multi-unit selling with conversions — cable bought by the drum and sold by the metre, bulbs bought by the carton and sold singly — with a unit picker showing the price for each unit. Trade and contractor price tiers applied automatically once a customer is attached. Line-level editing: quantity, unit, price and line discount. Cart-level discount with a permitted ceiling. Floor-price protection — a sale below the product’s floor price requires manager PIN approval and records the approver. Customer attachment by phone search, with quick customer creation at the counter. Hold and resume multiple parked sales. Keyboard shortcuts throughout. Out-of-stock and unknown-barcode handling that informs rather than blocks.
A3Payments and Receipts
Cash with denomination shortcuts and large change display; mobile money across MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash and AT Money with network selection, payer number, transaction reference and explicit received-confirmation; card and bank transfer with reference capture; and split payments across multiple methods with a running balance that will not complete until the amount due reaches zero. Receipt generation and printing to 80mm and 58mm thermal printers with an on-screen preview, configurable header, logo, footer and returns wording. Reprint of any past receipt. Print failure never blocks a sale — the sale is saved and printing can be retried. Receipt numbering is sequential per Outlet and per terminal, gapless and never reused.
A4Cashier Shifts and Cash Control
Opening a shift with a counted float; a blind close in which the cashier counts the drawer by denomination without seeing what the system expects; variance revealed only after submission, with a mandatory written explanation beyond a configurable threshold. Shift register showing outlet, cashier, times, sales total, expected cash, counted cash and variance. Cash movements in and out of the drawer — banking, owner drawings, supplier payments, float transfers — each with a reason and a user. End-of-day reconciliation per Outlet with takings broken down by payment method.
A5Product Catalogue
Full catalogue with images, SKUs, multiple barcodes, categories, brands and supplier links. Per-product selling units with conversion factors and a price for each unit. Four price points per product — cost, retail, trade and floor — with live margin calculation and optional per-Outlet overrides. Cost price hidden from users without permission. Price change history. Category, brand and unit-of-measure management. CSV import wizard with template download, column mapping, row-level validation, error reporting and a preview before commit. Bulk price updates across a filtered selection with an affected-count preview.
A6Inventory Control
Stock held per product per Outlet — there is no single global quantity anywhere in the System. Every change is a movement row carrying the Outlet, quantity delta, reason, user, timestamp and source document, so the current level always equals the sum of its movements. Goods receipt against a supplier with purchase units, unit costs and a confirmation step showing the resulting stock and the new weighted average cost. Stock adjustments with a mandatory reason, value impact and approval above a threshold. Damage and write-off recording. Low-stock list grouped by supplier with suggested reorder quantities. Stock valuation at cost by category and Outlet. Physical stock count with variance review. Stock transfer between Outlets. An exceptions register surfacing oversells, large adjustments and write-offs for review.
A7Sales History, Voids, Returns and Exchanges
Searchable transaction history filtered by date, receipt number, customer, amount, cashier and Outlet, with a full sale detail view showing lines, units, payments, cashier, terminal and any approver. Completed sales are immutable — correction is by void, which writes reversing stock movements and leaves both records visible, requires a reason and manager approval, and is logged. Returns and exchanges against an original receipt: select the lines and quantities being returned, record replacement items on a swap, calculate the balance due either way, and print a return voucher. Return and void registers exportable to CSV.
A8Installment Plans and Customer Credit
Creation of a written installment agreement against a customer, with the items, total value, deposit and payment schedule. Recording of each collection with a printed payment slip. Automatic identification of overdue plans, outstanding balances and credit ageing. Portfolio metrics showing total credit extended, collected and at risk. Full export to CSV. This addresses the single largest cash risk in hardware retail — goods released on trust and paid for later.
A9Customers and Suppliers
Customer register with phone-number search, customer type mapped to a price tier (retail, electrician, contractor), purchase history across both Outlets, total spent and purchase count. Supplier directory with contacts, payment terms, products supplied, goods-receipt history and total purchased.
A10Expenses and Profitability
Operational expense ledger by category and Outlet with dates and notes. Automatic calculation of gross revenue, cost of goods sold at the cost frozen on each sale line, gross profit, total operating expenditure and net profit or loss. Category breakdown of where the money is going. Because cost is frozen at the moment of sale, later price or cost changes never rewrite historical profit.
A11Dashboard and Business Reporting
A role-aware dashboard: the Owner sees both Outlets combined or separately with today’s sales, estimated profit, expected cash and stock value; the Manager sees one Outlet operationally; the Cashier sees only their own shift and their own sales, with no profit or stock value. Reports covering sales by day, product, category, cashier and customer; gross profit and margin; best sellers and slow movers; stock on hand and stock movements; payment method breakdown; the discount and override log; the void and refund log; and purchases by supplier. Every report is available per Outlet and combined, with date-range presets and CSV export.
A12Outlet Comparison and Analytics Workspace
Side-by-side comparison of the Outlets on revenue, margin, transaction count, average sale and stock position. An analytics workspace with six chart families — sales and margin velocity, comparative outlet performance, category mix, payment channel distribution, installment credit ageing and top-mover velocity — supported by an inference engine that reads the figures and writes a plain-language commentary: an executive summary, a financial health grade, an explanation of what each chart is showing and where the risks and opportunities sit. Preset questions cover margin comparison between the lighting and hardware lines, debtor and installment risk, fast versus dead stock, and payment method friction, and free-text questions can be asked of the data. The engine falls back to a deterministic rules model so the workspace continues to produce analysis when the network is unavailable.
A13Audit Trail
A read-only record of consequential actions — price changes, stock adjustments, voids, discounts and overrides, permission changes, user administration and settings changes — each with the user, the Outlet, the timestamp and the before and after values. Filterable by user, action, entity, Outlet and date. It cannot be edited or deleted from within the System by anyone, including the Owner.
A14System Settings and Administration
Store profile with legal identity, currency and rounding. Registration and management of branches with staff assignment, so further Outlets can be added without redevelopment. User accounts with credentials, PINs and Outlet assignment. The roles and permissions matrix. Manager PIN policy governing floor-price and void overrides. Hardware and receipt configuration with 80mm and 58mm templates and a test print. Theme and accent colour with a live preview. Backup and export — a complete JSON archive of the business and accounting CSV exports, available to the Client on demand.
A15Operating Characteristics
Global search across products, customers, suppliers and sales from any screen. Consistent loading, empty, error and permission states throughout. Amounts held in minor units as integers so rounding never drifts; quantities held to three decimal places to support cut lengths. Every sale carries a unique identifier so a double-click, retry or reconnection can never create a duplicate sale or a duplicate payment. Stock decrements happen inside a locked database transaction, so two terminals cannot sell the same last unit. Designed desktop-first for the counter, usable on a tablet in the store room, and readable on the Owner’s phone for the dashboard and reports.
